## Proposed mapping to app lessons (for the German trainer to confirm)

The pack plans about 48 lessons across A1–B1, so about 16 per level. These 18 topics fit A1 well. Proposed order: teaching order rather than list order, so that sein/haben and pronouns come before family and articles.

1 Greetings & introducing yourself · 2 Alphabet & spelling your name · 3 Numbers 0–100 · 4 Pronouns + sein/haben · 5 Introducing someone · 6 Articles (der/die/das) · 7 Family · 8 Questions (W-Fragen) · 9 Days, time & appointments · 10 Food & vegetables · 11 Colours · 12 Negation (kein/nicht) · 13 Accusative · 14 Modal verbs · 15 Home (Mein Haus) · 16 Weather · 17 Directions · then the **A1 checkpoint** (review).

That makes 17 lessons plus a checkpoint. Trade-specific packs (FR-306) sit on top of these.

## Issues to resolve (also in open-questions.md)
- **Rights to the videos.** These look like third-party videos. Embedding or hosting them in a government app needs written permission from the rights holder. YouTube embeds also need a network connection (which conflicts with offline use, NFR-05), and YouTube's terms don't allow downloading. Treat them as a **teaching reference for our own content**, not as in-app media, unless a licence is obtained.
- **Length.** The videos run 4–13 minutes. FR-305 says in-app video is "under 2 minutes for selected topics", and NFR-02 caps pack size. Our own short clips (or none) fit better.
